Zone Control

GET /{zone name}/{action}[/{parameter}]

Actions

Playback

  • play
  • pause
  • playpause toggles playing state
  • next
  • previous

Seek

  • seek/{seconds into track} alias: timeseek
  • timeseek/{seconds into track}
  • trackseek/{queue index}

Volume

  • volume/{absolute volume}
  • volume/{+ or -}{relative volume}
  • groupvolume/{absolute volume}
  • groupvolume/{+ or -}{relative volume}
  • mute
  • unmute
  • groupmute alias: mutegroup
  • groupunmute alias: unmutegroup
  • togglemute
  • lockvolumes
  • unlockvolumes experimental enforce the volume that was selected when locking!

Playback Settings

  • favorite/{favorite name} alias: favourite
  • playlist/{playlist name}
  • repeat/{on | off | one | toggle}
  • shuffle/{on | off | toggle}
  • crossfade/{on | off | toggle}

Queue

  • queue[/{limit}[/{offset}]][/detailed]
  • clearqueue
  • trackseek/{queue index}

Room Grouping

  • add/{player name from existing zone, prefix with player you want to join}
  • join/{player player to join in, prefix with player from current zone} (this is just the inverse of add)
  • isolate
  • ungroup (alias of isolate)
  • leave (alias of isolate)

Announcements

  • say/{phrase}[/{language code or voice}][/{announce volume}]
  • clip/{filename}[/{announce volume}]

Equalizer

  • nightmode/{on | off | toggle}
  • speechenhancement/{on | off | toggle}
  • bass/{-10 through 10}
  • treble/{-10 through 10}

Sources and Music Services

  • linein selects line-in for the zone
  • linein/{source player name} selects line-in from another player
  • sub/{on | off}
  • sub/gain/{value}
  • sub/crossover/{frequency in hz}
  • sub/polarity/{0 | 1}
  • spotify/{now | next | queue}/{spotify URI}
  • applemusic/{now | next | queue}/{song | album | playlist}:{id}
  • amazonmusic/{now | next | queue}/{song | album}:{id}
  • napster/{now | next | queue}/{song | album}:{id}
  • aldilifemusic/{now | next | queue}/{song | album}:{id}
  • tunein/{play | set}/{station id}
  • bbcsounds/{play | set}/{stream code}
  • siriusxm/{channel number or station name}
  • musicsearch/{apple | spotify | deezer | elite | library}/{album | song | station | playlist | load}[/{search term}]

Other

  • state returns a json-representation of the current state of player
  • setavtransporturi/{encoded URI}

Learn more at https://github.com/jishi/node-sonos-http-api
